E-commerce

E-commerce Website

Client project · Online store

Overview

A complete online store built for a client who wanted to sell products to customers around the clock. Shoppers can browse the catalogue, add items to their cart and pay securely, without ever leaving the site.

What I built

I designed the database, the storefront and the admin area. The owner can add products, update prices and stock, and track every order from a simple dashboard, with no technical help needed.

Key features

  • Product catalogue with categories and search
  • Shopping cart and smooth checkout
  • Secure online payments
  • Order tracking and stock management
  • Fully responsive on phones, tablets and desktops

School Management System

Client project · Web application

Overview

A complete platform that helps a school run its daily operations from one place. It replaces paper files and scattered spreadsheets with organised, searchable digital records.

I built separate dashboards for administrators, teachers and students, so each person sees only what they need. Staff save hours on routine paperwork, and records stay accurate and easy to find.

Key features

  • Student and staff records management
  • Class, subject and timetable setup
  • Attendance and result recording

  • Printable reports

Transport Management System

Client project · Web application

Overview

A system that gives a transport business a clear view of its vehicles, drivers and trips. It replaces manual logbooks with one reliable, always up-to-date record of daily operations.

What I built

I built the tools to register vehicles, assign drivers, schedule trips and record bookings. Management can see what is on the road and what is available at a glance, and plan the day with confidence.

Key features

  • Vehicle and driver registration
  • Trip scheduling and driver assignment
  • Booking and passenger records
  • Admin overview of fleet activity
  • Searchable history of past trips

Hotel Management System

Client project · Booking platform

Overview

A luxury hotel website that lets guests search for rooms, see live availability and book online. The design is dark and elegant with gold accents, matching the feel of a five-star stay.

What I built

I built the public website and the back office behind it. Staff can manage rooms, upload homepage slider images, run special offers and follow every reservation, while guests pay online with Paystack or Flutterwave.

Key features

  • Room search by dates, guests and room type
  • Live availability and instant booking
  • Online payments with Paystack and Flutterwave
  • Admin-managed rooms, slider images and offers
  • Guest accounts and a management dashboard
